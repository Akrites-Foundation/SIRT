# Conflict of Interest and Recusal Policy

**Type:** Policy
**Approver:** SIRT Director
**Owner:** Akrites SIRT
**Status:** Draft — for Akrites SIRT review
**Applies to:** Everyone who takes part in an Akrites SIRT case or decision: SIRT staff, the SIRT Director, Working Group (WG) Chairs and members, read-in participants, and Governing Board members when they act on a case
**Related:** [Read-In Policy](./read-in-policy.md) (§2 Gate 3, exclusion check) · [MoLR Policy](./molr-policy.md) (§5.2) · [WG Governance Policy](./wg-governance-policy.md) · [Code of Conduct](../../Code_of_Conduct.md)

---

## 1. Purpose

Akrites handles vulnerabilities that affect its own Members' products, and the people who work on a case often have an interest in the outcome. An interest is not wrongdoing. It becomes a problem only when it is hidden or when the person with the interest also makes the decision. This policy requires people to declare interests and keeps conflicted people out of the decisions they could influence.

## 2. What is a conflict of interest

A person has a conflict of interest in a case when any of these apply and could reasonably be seen to affect their judgment:

- **Commercial:** their employer, or a client or competitor of their employer, makes, sells or depends on the affected product, package or service, or stands to gain or lose from the timing or content of the disclosure.
- **Personal:** a close personal or family relationship with the Finder, the maintainer or someone at an affected organization, or a financial interest in an affected organization.
- **Role:** the person is the maintainer of the affected project, or is the Finder or a co-finder of the vulnerability.
- **Competitive or legal sensitivity:** a competitive or antitrust concern, or a sanctions or legal bar, as covered by the exclusion check in the [Read-In Policy](./read-in-policy.md).

Being a Member's engineer, or the maintainer of the affected project, does not on its own bar someone from helping on a case. It must be declared, and the rules below decide what they may do.

## 3. Declare it

1. **Declare early.** Anyone who has or may have a conflict declares it to the SIRT as soon as they know, and before they are read in to the case or take part in a decision on it.
2. **Record it.** The SIRT case coordinator records the declaration in the case read-in log (or, for a decision outside a case, in the decision record).
3. **When unsure, declare.** A declaration that turns out to be unnecessary costs nothing.
4. **Update it.** A person whose circumstances change during a case declares the change promptly.

The SIRT also asks about conflicts in the exclusion check before every read-in.

## 4. Recuse from decisions

A conflicted person **recuses** from these, and does not influence them by other means:

- approving read-ins, tiers or exceptions for the case;
- setting or changing the disclosure timeline or severity;
- approving a Maintainer of Last Resort engagement or release ([MoLR Policy §5.2](./molr-policy.md#52-approval-authority--recusal));
- escalation decisions and the content of advisories that concern their own or their employer's product.

A conflicted person **may** supply technical information when asked, do analysis or patch work that others review, and take part as a Member of the WG, unless the SIRT decides that the conflict is serious enough to exclude them under the exclusion check. They never approve their own work, and they never sign off their own releases.

## 5. Who decides in each case

| Conflicted person | Who takes the decision instead |
|---|---|
| SIRT staff (case coordinator, Incident Commander) | Another SIRT staff member, or the SIRT Director |
| WG member | The WG Chair, who tells the SIRT; the SIRT may exclude the member from the case |
| WG Chair | The SIRT Director, or the Technical PgM Director, who may designate an Interim Chair for the decision |
| SIRT Director | The Technical PgM Director |
| Governing Board member (acting on a case or exception) | The Board decides without that member, and the Chair or a Co-Chair records the recusal |
| Both the SIRT Director and the Technical PgM Director | The Governing Board Chair or a Co-Chair appoints a decider |

## 6. Questions and challenges

Anyone who believes a conflict has not been declared, or has been handled badly, can raise it with the SIRT Director, or with the Technical PgM Director if the concern is about the Director. A concern about a Governing Board member goes to the Chair or a Co-Chair. A concern is reviewed promptly and the outcome is recorded. Raising a concern in good faith is never held against the person who raised it.

## 7. Failure to declare

Not declaring a known conflict, or acting on a decision despite a conflict, is a breach of this policy. The SIRT may remove the person from the case, and a breach by a read-in participant is handled under [Read-In Policy §8](./read-in-policy.md) (revocation). A repeated or serious breach is reported to the Governing Board.

## 8. Records and review

The case read-in log and decision records are kept with the case. The SIRT reviews this policy at least once a year, and after any case where a conflict was disputed.
